About

Alex Abian

Dubai-based photographer working with luxury hotels, architecture firms, and brands that value precision over styling. My work sits between editorial and documentary — natural light, structural composition, unforced moments. Hospitality is my primary focus, but I shoot across editorial, commercial, and brand campaigns as well.

Previous clients include Van Moof, NTT / Meson Inc., Petit Pli, and editorial publications. Previously based in Tokyo; now operating out of Dubai for the GCC, Europe, and Asia. Fluent in English, Spanish, and Japanese — trilingual communication is standard on every project.

Available for hospitality shoots, architectural documentation, editorial assignments, and brand campaigns worldwide.
