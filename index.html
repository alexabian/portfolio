What camera do you shoot on?

Hasselblad X2D 100C medium format — 100 megapixels. For video, Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera 4K and 6K. Natural light prioritised throughout. Every image is captured and delivered at full 100MP resolution.

Do you travel for assignments?

Yes. Based in Dubai, available worldwide — GCC, Europe, and Asia. Pre-shoot site visits are included for all projects, regardless of location.

What's your turnaround time?

Standard delivery is 7 business days from the shoot date. Rush delivery is available for award submissions and urgent deadlines — additional fee applies. All files delivered as 100MP TIFFs and optimised JPEGs.

Do you sell presets and LUTs?

Yes. Lightroom preset packs ($24–$34) and DaVinci Resolve LUT packs ($39–$49). All LUTs are optimised for Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera 4K and 6K workflows. .cube format for universal compatibility.

What file resolution do you deliver?

100MP medium format RAW files from the Hasselblad X2D 100C. Final images delivered as 100MP 16-bit TIFFs for print and optimised high-quality JPEGs for digital and web use.
