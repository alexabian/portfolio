Dubai Photographer — Architectural, Hospitality & Editorial

Alex Abian is a Dubai-based photographer specialising in architectural photography, hospitality photography, and editorial photography for luxury hotels, architecture firms, and commercial brands across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the wider GCC region. Previously based in Tokyo, Alex brings a trilingual (English, Spanish, Japanese) perspective to every project. Services include hotel interior and exterior photography, architectural documentation, lifestyle photography, food photography, commercial brand campaigns, and aerial photography. Alex also creates professional colour tools: Lightroom presets (Desert, Cinema, Monochrome packs) and DaVinci Resolve LUTs (Rec709 Cine-Grade, Film Emulation, Commercial packs) optimised for Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera 4K and 6K workflows. Available for assignments in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Doha, and worldwide.
