## Key Observations

1. **No one publishes pricing for high-end work** — transparency = commodity; bespoke = premium
2. **All use white/minimal sites** — the images ARE the design, the frame recedes
3. **Client badges / approvals** are the primary social proof (Marriott approved, etc.)
4. **Architecture and hospitality are separate niches** that overlap
5. **Nobody has an integrated preset/LUT store** — this is a genuine differentiator
6. **Portfolio sites are extremely minimal** — no dark themes, no orange accents, no fancy UI
7. **The real differentiator is client list and awards**, not website design
